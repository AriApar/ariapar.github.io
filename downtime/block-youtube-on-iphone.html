Choose when YouTube is available

A schedule lets you keep access to YouTube while blocking it during focused time.

  • Block it during work hours to reduce interruptions.
  • Keep it unavailable during study sessions.
  • Set an evening cutoff to protect your sleep.

Keep tutorials available outside focus time

YouTube can be useful for lessons, workouts, and repairs, so a permanent block may be too restrictive. Schedule the block around the work you need to finish, then use YouTube normally when that time ends.

Add youtube.com to the same rule if you switch from the app to Safari when the app is blocked.
