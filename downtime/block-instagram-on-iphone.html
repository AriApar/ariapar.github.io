How to block Instagram on iPhone on a schedule.

Keep Instagram installed, but block it automatically during work, study, evenings, or sleep. Start free with one rule, then choose monthly or lifetime Pro for more control.

See how Downtime works

Block Instagram automatically

Choose Instagram with Apple's Screen Time picker, set the days and times, and Downtime will apply the block automatically.

How to do it with Downtime

1

Create a schedule

Set the hours when Instagram should be unavailable, such as 9 AM to 1 PM for study or 10 PM to 7 AM for sleep.

2

Select Instagram

Use Apple's built-in picker to choose the Instagram app as part of the block rule.

3

Pick the days

Apply the block on weekdays, weekends, or every day.

4

Let the schedule handle it

When the block starts, Instagram is shielded automatically. You do not need to remember to switch anything on.

Keep Instagram, with clear limits

A schedule lets you use Instagram when you choose while keeping it blocked during the hours you want to protect.

  • Set an evening cutoff to protect your sleep.
  • Block it during study sessions to stay focused.
  • Keep it unavailable throughout your workday.

If you use Instagram for messages or work

Choose a narrower schedule instead of blocking the whole day. For example, block Instagram during a two-hour study session or after your usual bedtime, then let access return automatically.

If you also open Instagram in Safari, add instagram.com to the same rule so the website follows the app schedule.
