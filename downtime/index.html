Scheduled blocking

Set it once.
Let it repeat.

Work hours, study sessions, or a bedtime cutoff. Give distracting apps and websites a place in your day, then let Downtime handle the boundary.

  1. Create a rule. Choose its days and time ranges.
  2. Choose what to block. Select apps, categories, and websites with Apple’s Screen Time picker.
  3. Let it run. Blocking works even when Downtime is closed.

Start with one rule for free. Pro adds multiple rules for different parts of your day.

Essentials Mode Pro

Keep what you need.
Quiet the rest.

Keep messages, maps, and the apps you choose available. Essentials Mode blocks other apps so your phone can stay useful without pulling you back in.

Start it manually or schedule it for evenings, work, or weekends. Scheduled Essentials Mode takes priority over regular rules; those rules resume afterward.

Room for a short break

Pause blocking for five minutes. Your block resumes automatically. With Pro, choose a daily allowance: none, 1, 2, 3, 5, or unlimited pauses.

Strict Mode Pro

Protect your rules and pause settings from impulsive changes. Switching Strict Mode off starts a one-hour waiting period.

Backup & restore Pro

Export your configuration to a file and import it when you need to restore your setup.
